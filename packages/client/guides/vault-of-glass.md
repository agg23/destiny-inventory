## Encounters

1. The Spire
2. Confluxes

Defend Confluxes in each of the 3 lanes across 3 waves. You will first defend a single conflux, then two, then all three.

**Challenge Mode:** Wait to kill Wyverns until they begin their sacrificing animation at a Conflux.

3. Oracles

Blue Vex cubes (Oracles) will appear at pre-determined positions in a random order. When they appear, a chime will also play. The game will display the complete pattern for you twice, then you must destroy each Oracle in the same order they appeared.

**Challenge Mode:** A player cannot shoot the same Oracle more than once.
